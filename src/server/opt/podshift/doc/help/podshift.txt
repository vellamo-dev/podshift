podshift: manages the PodShift server.

Usage:
    podshift [--plain] <command> [arguments]
    podshift --help [<command>]
    podshift --version

Options:
    --help, -h      Show this help, or the help for one command.
    --version, -v   Show the PodShift, Podman and host versions.
    --plain         Plain output, without colors or spinner.
